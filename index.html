The Nurse and the Night Shift

Hollis Nwankwo has run the same emergency department desk for eleven years. For the last three, a triage model has scored every patient before they reach it.

We talk about having an opinion of someone you haven't met yet — and why, at two in the morning, giving that number up is unthinkable.

About the show

The other conversation about AI

Most of what gets said about artificial intelligence is said by people with something to launch. This is the other conversation: one guest at a time, someone who didn't choose this technology but now works next to it every day. A nurse. A teacher. An interpreter. We ask what changed, in ordinary detail, and we stay long enough to get past the first answer.

Episodes run forty to sixty minutes and are edited lightly. Nothing is scripted. We keep the pauses in, because the pause is usually where someone decides whether to tell you the true version.

The rule

No guest is asked to predict the future. Everyone is asked what happened on a specific day. If the answer would work at a conference, we asked the wrong question.

Know someone whose job changed and who hasn't been asked about it? That's who we want.
